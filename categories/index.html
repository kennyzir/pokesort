The capacity-only Only Bug predicate made the 14-day Daily scheduling constraint feasible. Infinite advertises and intentionally schedules eight families; it does not count this additive predicate as a ninth family, although its solver still evaluates the complete 101-predicate model for ambiguity.

How to use this reference

Test an exact fact against all 16 cards, then check whether the remaining 12 can still form three complete groups. A quartet can satisfy more than one label; the intended answer is the only complete four-group member partition, as verified by the solver.

Evidence boundary

The versioned model covers 1,025 National Pokédex species and 101 enumerable predicates from pinned PokéAPI data. It deliberately does not infer alternate-form typing, moves, abilities, evolution methods, starters, fossils, Ultra Beasts, pseudo-legendary status, or other community conventions. See data methodology and corrections.
