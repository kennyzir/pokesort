What is PokeSort?

PokeSort is a free daily Pokémon sorting puzzle. Each board gives you 16 Pokémon and four hidden categories. Your task is to identify the exact four groups before you use all four mistakes. Categories draw on source-backed facts such as default-form typing, introduction generation, Pokédex color, evolution-chain topology, and verified species flags.

The Daily puzzle is shared by every player and changes once per day. Infinite mode is available for practice without affecting your daily result. No account or download is required.
