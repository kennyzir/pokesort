What is PokeSort?

PokeSort is a free daily Pokémon sorting puzzle. Each board gives you 16 Pokémon and four hidden categories. Your task is to identify the exact four groups before you use all four mistakes. Categories draw on familiar Pokédex knowledge such as elemental typing, generation, evolution, regional origin, and special classifications.

The Daily puzzle is shared by every player and changes once per day. Infinite mode is available for practice without affecting your daily result. No account or download is required.

Looking for Pokelike’s Daily Pokésort? That is the six-Pokémon ordering minigame inside Pokelike, not this 4×4 board. Read the Pokelike Pokésort rules and use the spoiler-free worksheet.
