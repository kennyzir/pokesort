No signup and no long introduction: open today’s board, group 16 Pokémon by four exact connections, then continue in Infinite mode.

PokeSort can mean two different puzzle mechanics. Pokelike includes a Daily Pokésort that orders six Pokémon across five neighbouring links. This site plays a separate 4×4 grouping game. See the Pokelike Pokésort guide for the parent-game relationship and six-link rules.
