Plain-language policy

Privacy

MonSort does not require an account. Puzzle state is stored in your browser’s local storage so your progress survives a refresh.

Data on your device

Daily progress, infinite puzzle number, solved groups, and remaining mistakes are stored locally. You can remove them by clearing this site’s storage in your browser.

Hosting and analytics

The hosting provider may process standard request logs such as IP address, browser type, and requested URL for security and reliability. If privacy-friendly analytics are added later, this notice will be updated before collection begins.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project’s public GitHub repository.
