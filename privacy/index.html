PLAIN-LANGUAGE POLICY

Privacy

PokeSort does not require an account. Puzzle state is saved in browser local storage so a refresh does not erase your board.

Data on your device

Daily progress, Infinite puzzle number, solved groups, mistakes, and streak dates are stored locally. Clear this site’s storage to remove them.

Hosting

The hosting provider may process standard request logs such as IP address, browser type, and requested URL for security and reliability.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project’s public GitHub repository.
